AURA WALLPAPER

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

AURA WALLPAPER stores appearance preferences, favorites and wallpaper history on your device. Downloaded images, videos and depth layers are saved in app-private storage.

The app retrieves published wallpaper records and media from Supabase and the media hosts listed in those records. If an account session is active, favorites may synchronize with Supabase.

Advertising
AURA integrates Google AdMob. When advertising is available and the consent checks permit ad requests, the Google Mobile Ads SDK can collect and share IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. IP addresses can indicate approximate location. AURA does not request GPS location for advertising.

Google's User Messaging Platform checks whether a privacy message is required. Where available, use Ad privacy choices in Profile to review or change your choices. Android's advertising privacy settings also provide device-level controls. Read Google's advertising information at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

AURA does not request access to your contacts or private photo library. Save to Gallery creates the selected image in Pictures/AURA THEMES. Sharing opens Android's share sheet and shares what you select.

You can remove offline media in Downloads and reset local app data through Android settings. Your current system wallpaper may remain after deleting an offline copy. Google and Supabase manage data processed by their services under their respective policies.
